Private massage planning for Canary Wharf

Canary Wharf is a high-rise business and residential district with controlled office and apartment entrances. A corporate wellness visit and an in-room residential massage are operationally different requests.

Your hotel, home or workplace

Office wellness may require security clearance, loading approval and a permitted meeting room. Private apartment buildings may have reception desks, access lifts or short visitor-registration windows; address these before the visit.

Treatments to enquire about

A post-work relaxation or focused massage can be requested for a suitable residence. Corporate chair massage or team wellness needs numbers, room dimensions and a scheduled programme rather than a generic hotel appointment.
